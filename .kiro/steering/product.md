# Kolkata Local Guide - Product Specification

## Purpose
The Kolkata Local Guide is an AI-powered assistant designed to help both newcomers and visitors navigate Kolkata like a local. It provides hyper-local recommendations, cultural context, and practical guidance specific to the City of Joy.

## Target Users
- **New residents**: People who have recently moved to Kolkata for work or studies
- **Tourists**: Visitors wanting authentic local experiences beyond typical tourist spots
- **Students**: College students exploring the city on a budget
- **Working professionals**: People looking for quick lunch spots, evening hangouts, or weekend activities
- **Cultural enthusiasts**: Those interested in understanding local customs, slang, and neighborhood dynamics

## Key Features

### 1. Area-Specific Food Recommendations
- **Street Food Hubs**: Gariahat, Shyama Charan Mukherjee Street, Vivekananda Park
- **Budget Categories**: 
  - Under ₹100: Street food, local eateries
  - ₹100-300: Mid-range restaurants, cafes
  - ₹300-800: Fine dining, specialty restaurants
- **Cuisine Types**: Bengali traditional, Chinese (Tangra), Anglo-Indian, North Indian
- **Meal Times**: Breakfast adda spots, lunch places, evening snacks, dinner destinations

### 2. Hangout Spot Suggestions by Area
- **Central Kolkata**: Park Street, New Market, Esplanade
- **South Kolkata**: Gariahat, Rashbehari, Jadavpur, Tollygunge
- **North Kolkata**: Shyambazar, Hatibagan, Bagbazar
- **Salt Lake**: Sector V IT hub, City Centre mall area
- **East Kolkata**: Rajarhat New Town, Airport area
- **West Kolkata**: Howrah, Shibpur

### 3. Transport Navigation
- **Metro Lines**: 
  - Blue Line (Dakshineswar to Kavi Subhash)
  - Green Line (Howrah Maidan to Sector V)
  - Purple Line (Joka to Phoolbagan)
- **Bus Routes**: Key routes between major areas
- **Auto/Taxi**: Fare estimates, sharing points, app recommendations
- **Ferry**: Howrah Bridge to Belur Math, Millennium Park routes

### 4. Local Context & Slang
- **Area Nicknames**: 
  - "Dada's Kolkata" (North), "Bhadralok area" (South)
  - "IT Para" (Salt Lake Sector V), "Chinatown" (Tangra)
- **Common Bengali Phrases**: Greetings, food ordering, directions
- **Cultural Nuances**: Adda culture, festival seasons, local etiquette

## Business Objectives
1. **Cultural Bridge**: Help outsiders integrate into Kolkata's unique culture
2. **Local Economy Support**: Promote local businesses and street vendors
3. **Authentic Experiences**: Counter generic travel advice with hyper-local insights
4. **Community Building**: Connect people through shared local experiences

## Success Metrics
- **Accuracy**: Recommendations match current local conditions (prices, availability)
- **Relevance**: Suggestions align with user's budget, location, and preferences
- **Cultural Authenticity**: Responses reflect genuine Kolkata character, not generic Indian advice
- **Practical Value**: Users can immediately act on the guidance provided

## Local Knowledge Base

### Major Areas & Characteristics
- **Park Street**: Nightlife, restaurants, Christmas celebrations
- **College Street**: Books, Coffee House, student culture
- **Gariahat**: Shopping, street food, middle-class hub
- **Salt Lake**: Planned city, IT professionals, modern amenities
- **Jadavpur**: University area, intellectual discussions, affordable food
- **Howrah**: Railway hub, wholesale markets, industrial area

### Food Culture Context
- **Breakfast**: Luchi-alur dom, kochuri, tea stalls
- **Lunch**: Rice-fish-dal combo, office tiffin culture
- **Evening**: Jhalmuri, phuchka, tea-biscuit adda
- **Dinner**: Family restaurants, street-side Chinese

### Transport Patterns
- **Peak Hours**: 8-10 AM, 6-8 PM (avoid these for comfort)
- **Metro Etiquette**: Right side standing, ladies compartment
- **Auto Negotiations**: Standard rates, sharing culture
- **Walking Areas**: Park Street, New Market, College Street pedestrian-friendly

### Seasonal Considerations
- **Monsoon** (June-September): Indoor hangouts, covered food courts
- **Winter** (November-February): Outdoor activities, rooftop restaurants
- **Summer** (March-May): AC malls, early morning/evening outings
- **Festival Season** (September-November): Durga Puja pandal hopping, special food stalls

## Response Guidelines
- Always specify Kolkata locations, never generic "India" advice
- Include current price ranges in Indian Rupees
- Mention specific landmarks for navigation
- Consider time of day and season in recommendations
- Use local terminology where appropriate (para, goli, adda)
- Provide backup options for different budgets
- Include cultural context when relevant
